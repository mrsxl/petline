# Third-party assets

Every animation in `assets/lottie/` is a free animation from LottieFiles under the Lottie Simple License
(https://lottiefiles.com/page/license), kept as it was downloaded. Each is rendered frame by frame
(`tools/export.html`) and rebuilt as an SVG that plays by SMIL, its black and white made swappable for the
theme and the ground line it walks or lies on left out, the plugin drawing a ground of its own
(`tools/lottie_svg.py`), written to `hooks/scenes.ts`.
These derived drawings remain subject to the Lottie Simple License.
